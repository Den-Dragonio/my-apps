About the Project:

Morse Translator is a lightweight yet powerful utility for instant encoding and decoding of messages. The program uses Python algorithms to accurately translate text into sequences of dots and dashes, and vice versa.

Perfect for learning Morse code, encrypting secret messages, or simply understanding the logic behind string algorithms.

App Advantages:

  • ✓
    Educational Effect: The app helps train your ear and memorize the rhythmic pattern of Morse code, rather than simply translating text.
  • ✓
    Eye Comfort: A full dark theme allows you to work with text at night without straining your eyes.
  • ✓
    Fully Autonomous: The program works completely offline. No ads, trackers, or internet requirements.
  • ✓
    Customizable Speed: You control the audio playback speed (WPM), adjusting it to your skill level.
