Arcana Oracle

  • Engine: Python + Kivy
  • Installation: Portable (Start.bat)

System Requirements:

OS: Windows 10 / 11

Internet: Required once (to download libraries)

Launch: Automatic via .bat file

About the App:

Arcana Oracle is a unique combination of ancient esoteric knowledge and the modern power of Python. The app features an incredibly high-quality visual style powered by the Kivy framework.

At the core of the program lies a complex computational formula. The reading result is not random — it's a mathematical calculation that factors in the Moon phase, Chinese calendar year, card suit combinations, and the Major Arcana. All you need to do is enter your date of birth.

Key Features:

  • 🔮
    4 Spheres of Fate: Choose one of 4 segments — Family, Finance, Love, and Success — for your reading. The complex formula accounts for the Moon phase, Chinese calendar year, and numerology.
  • 🃏
    Flexible Spreads: Choose the number of cards: from 1 to 5. Lightning-fast result calculation with no delays.
  • 🎨
    3 Legendary Decks: The app includes the most authoritative systems: Rider-Waite, Marseille Tarot, and Thoth Tarot. Switch between them in one click.
  • ⚙️
    Smart Launch: Start.bat will automatically create a virtual environment and install all required libraries. Safe for your system.

How to Install:

  1. Download and extract the archive.
  2. Find the start.bat file and run it.
  3. Wait for the library installation to finish (the black console window). This is only needed on the first launch.
  4. The app will open automatically. Enjoy your reading!
