About the App:

Gym Notebook is your personal digital assistant. All workout data is stored in a reliable database. You can always see the date and time of each entry, and the flexible settings system lets you tailor the app to your needs.

Features:

  • 📅
    User-Friendly Interface: A convenient and intuitive calendar. Currently optimized for 2025, but you can freely select any month and any day to add entries.
  • 🏋️
    Workout System: Enter the name of your training split (Upper/Lower, Push/Pull, etc.). Full freedom in naming your days.
  • 🔢
    Exercise Flexibility: Add any number of exercises (from 1 to infinity). Adjust the number of reps performed for each set with precision down to a single rep.
  • 📝
    Notes & Database: Leave comments and notes for each workout (how you felt, working weights). All data, including date and time, is reliably saved in the database. A settings system is also implemented.

🔜 Global Development Roadmap:

🛠 Workout Improvements:

  • 🟠 Extended Comments: A notes window with text formatting (bold, italic) under each exercise.
  • 🟠 Time Management: A field to log workout duration and a rest timer between sets.
  • 🟠 Templates: Ability to copy past workouts or create presets (e.g., "Leg Day").

📅 Navigation & History:

  • 🟠 Calendar Expansion: Adding an archive for 2024 and support for future years (2026+).
  • 🟠 Sidebar: Quick access to the last 5 workouts (as blocks, like in the calendar).
  • 🟠 Quick Navigation: Buttons to jump to "First workout" and "Latest entry".

⚙️ System Settings:

  • 🟠 Conversion: A fully functional kg/lbs toggle that recalculates all values in the database.
  • 🟠 Theming: Working theme switcher (Light / Dark / System) and interface language selector.
  • 🟠 Weather: Integration of a weather widget to log workout conditions.

👤 Account & Platform:

  • 🟠 My Profile: A full-featured tab with body measurements (anthropometrics) and personal statistics.
  • 🟠 Multi-User: A login and registration system for multiple users to use the app.
  • 🟠 iOS Support: Porting and support for Apple devices.
