Connect4 3D

  • Engine: Unity 3D
  • Platform: Web (Browser) / Windows
  • Genre: Logic / Board Game
  • Controls: Mouse + Keyboard

System Requirements (PC):

OS: Windows 10 / 11 (64-bit)

RAM: 2-4 GB

Web: Any modern browser (Chrome, Edge)

About the Game:

Connect4 3D is the classic logic duel, recreated on the powerful Unity engine. Preserving the spirit of the original, modern visuals, handy analysis tools, and satisfying physics have been added.

The main goal is to form a line of 4 pieces of your color (vertically, horizontally, or diagonally). The game offers a convenient interface with move history and highlighting so you can focus on strategy.

Functional Features:

  • 🎯
    Smart Highlight (Ghost Rod): When hovering the mouse over a column, it lights up yellow. This eliminates misclicks — you always know where your piece will land.
  • 📝
    Move List (History Log): A dedicated UI panel that records every move. Great for game analysis and tracking mistakes.
  • ✨
    Victory Effect: Once a line is complete, the game automatically highlights the winning combination in yellow so the final result is unmistakable.
  • 🔄
    Quick Restart: Made a mistake or want to play again? Simply press the "R" key for an instant match restart.
  • 📐
    Clear Grid: A clear letter-and-number grid with visual dividers helps you navigate 3D space, so you always know exactly where you're placing your piece.

📜 Game Rules:

  1. Players take turns dropping pieces into one of 16 columns.
  2. A piece always falls to the lowest free cell.
  3. The first player to form a line of 4 pieces wins in their color.
  4. The line can be: horizontal, vertical, or diagonal.
  5. If all cells are filled and no line of 4 exists — it's a draw.

🔜 Development Plans (Roadmap):

  • 🔴 Advanced AI: Integration of bots with difficulty levels (Beginner, Amateur, Grandmaster).
  • 🔴 Stockfish-style Analysis: Real-time position evaluation and post-game analysis (advantage bar), like in chess.
  • 🔴 Network Play: Multiplayer over local network (LAN) and global online.
  • 🔴 Rating System: Leagues, ranks, and a leaderboard (MMR / ELO).
  • 🔴 Customization: Skins for pieces and the board (neon, metal, wood, etc.).
  • 🔴 Turn Indicator: Display whose turn it is to avoid confusion.
  • 🔴 Replay System: Ability to download or upload a move list to review a played match.
