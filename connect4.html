About the Remake (Lua + LÖVE2D):

Connect 4 — 3D has been completely rebuilt from the ground up in Lua on the LÖVE2D engine with a custom 3D software-projected mesh pipeline and procedural GLSL shaders. On a 4×4×4 cubic board (16 vertical pegs, 64 total cells, and 76 possible winning lines), every move becomes a spatial puzzle across rows, columns, and 3D diagonals.

Compared to the original prototype, this edition introduces 3-tier Alpha-Beta AI bots, real-time Online ELO matchmaking backed by Firebase, Friends & 6-character Room Invites, Swiss & Knockout Tournaments with coin prize pools, LAN multiplayer, interactive Move Rewind & Match Replays, and a full 3D Shop filled with custom piece skins, themed boards, synthesized material sound effects, and adaptive UI themes.

Key Modes & Gameplay Features:

🤖 3-Difficulty AI Bots

Challenge Easy, Medium, or Hard bots powered by Alpha-Beta pruning across all 76 winning lines of the 4×4×4 cube. Pick White, Black, or Random starting color and earn daily coin rewards.

🌐 Ranked Online & ELO

Create an account or play as a Guest. Ranked matches track 4 separate ELO ratings (Standard Bullet 1m, Standard Blitz 5m, Free Bullet 1m, Free Blitz 5m) with live ELO history charts and 60s rematches.

🏆 Swiss & Knockout Tournaments

Create or join 4, 8, or 16-player tournaments via room code in Swiss or Knockout Bracket format — play against real players or bots with optional Host Fund or Entry Fee coin prize pools.

👥 Friends & Local Network (LAN)

Add up to 50 friends, see online/in-match presence, and invite friends directly via 6-character room codes. Prefer offline Wi-Fi? Host or join instant peer-to-peer matches on LAN UDP Port 24444.

⏪ Rewind & Match Replay

Every move is logged in 3D coordinate notation (e.g., a1.1). Step forward or backward through any live board or re-watch past matches directly from your Profile Match History.

🔊 Synthesized 3D Audio

Every board material features custom synthesized sound physics — warm walnut wood, crisp ash wood, forest rustle, resonant steel/gold clinks, obsidian stone clicks, sweet cake crunch, and billiard ball clacks.

Controls Guide:

Connect 4 — 3D keyboard, mouse, and touch controls
Input / Control Action
Left Click / Tap on Peg Place a piece onto the highlighted peg (Standard Gravity or Free Height)
Mouse Drag / WASD / Touch Drag Rotate the 3D camera smoothly around the board
Mouse Wheel / Z, X / Pinch Zoom camera In & Out (scrolling over the Move Log scrolls moves)
Arrow Keys (← / → / ↑ / ↓) Rewind, step forward, jump to start, or jump to latest move
F (after match ends) Hide or show the result overlay to inspect the final 3D position
R / Esc Quick restart match (R) · Return to Main Menu or close modal (Esc)

📜 Game Rules (4×4×4):

  1. The board consists of 16 vertical pegs in a 4×4 grid (columns a–d, rows 1–4), each holding up to 4 pieces vertically (levels 1–4).
  2. White always moves first. Players alternate placing one piece per turn (notation: a1.1).
  3. In Standard Mode, gravity drops your piece to the lowest open slot on a peg; in Free Mode, you can place at any empty 3D slot.
  4. First player to align 4 pieces of their color along any of the 76 straight lines (horizontal, vertical, flat diagonal, vertical diagonal, or 3D space diagonal) wins!
  5. If all 64 cells are filled with no line of 4, the match ends in a draw.
