System Requirements:

OS: Windows 10/11, macOS, or Linux (for desktop builds)

Web Browser: Any modern browser supporting WebGL & WebAssembly (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge)

RAM: 64 MB (Web) / 256 MB (Desktop)

About the Game:

Surv2D is a high-intensity, retro-styled 2D top-down survival game built from the ground up in Lua. Players are thrown into a post-apocalyptic environment where they must gather resources, craft upgraded weaponry and armor, fortify base camps, and fight off relentless waves of zombies.

The core experience revolves around the dynamic day/night cycle. During the day, players explore, collect wood and stone, and upgrade their base. When night falls, aggressive zombie hordes begin their assault, testing the durability of the base's walls and automated defense turrets.

Key Gameplay Mechanics:

  • 🧟
    Zombie Waves & Boss Battles: Fend off waves of zombies with scaling health, speed, and intelligence. Confront powerful boss monsters that test your combat precision and gear preparation.
  • 🏰
    Base Fortification: Construct defensive structures, including wooden and stone walls, gates, and automatic crossbow turrets that lock onto targets and thin out approaching hordes.
  • ⚔️
    Equipment Progression: Gear up with helmets, chestplates, boots, and up to three passive rings. Collect materials to forge copper, iron, or mythril weapons and level up your combat stats.
  • 💎
    Forge & Enchanting: Refine materials at the interactive Forge and unlock passive player bonuses (such as combat speed, health recovery, or double strikes) using the Enchanting Table.
  • ☀️
    Day & Night Cycle: Enemies become faster and deal more damage in the dark. Campfires and beds scattered around the map serve as safe havens to restore health and stamina when morning arrives.

Controls Guide:

Input Key Action / Command
W, A, S, D Movement (Up / Left / Down / Right)
Shift Sprint (Consumes stamina)
Mouse Aim weapon or cursor
; or Ж / Left Click Action Key (Attack, Shoot, Build structure)

E Interact (Open chests, use Forge, or use Enchanting table)
1 - 9 Select hotbar item

🔜 Roadmap & Updates:

✅ Implemented Features:

  • ✔️ A* Pathfinding: Intelligent search behavior for zombies traversing bases.
  • ✔️ Day/Night Cycle: Gradual lighting shaders and speed-up thresholds at dusk.
