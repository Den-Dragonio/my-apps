About the App:

Books Review App is your personal digital hub for organizing everything you've read. The tool is designed for those who want not just to consume content, but to analyze it. Build your own library of impressions from manga, manhwa, books, comics, and visual novels in a single, intuitive interface.

The app is focused on a User-Friendly experience: minimalist design combined with powerful database functionality.

Key Features:

  • ★
    Flexible Rating System: Ability to rate in the classic "star" format as well as a precise numeric value (e.g., 8/10).
  • 📚
    Progress Tracking: Log the number of chapters or pages read. You'll always know where you left off.
  • 🏷️
    Tagging & Sorting: Assign tags to titles for quick filtering and searching your library.
  • ✍️
    Reviews & Notes: Dedicated fields for writing a synopsis, brief summary, and capturing your emotional response after reading.
  • 🛡️
    Security & Administration: Built-in access rights system. Editing, adding, and deleting reviews is only available to the administrator (you). Guests can only view.
